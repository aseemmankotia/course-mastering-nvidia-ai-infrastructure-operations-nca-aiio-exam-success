### Multiple Choice

**Q1.** A candidate is preparing their time management strategy for the NCA-AIIO exam. The exam consists of 50 questions to be completed in 60 minutes. What is the average time the candidate should allocate per question to complete the exam on time?

A) 60 seconds per question
B) 72 seconds per question
C) 90 seconds per question
D) 45 seconds per question

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct: B**

72 seconds per question is the correct average time (60 minutes × 60 seconds = 3,600 seconds ÷ 50 questions = 72 seconds). Option A (60 seconds) would leave 10 minutes unused. Option C (90 seconds) would require 75 minutes total, exceeding the time limit. Option D (45 seconds) is too rushed and would finish in only 37.5 minutes.

</details>

**Q3.** During the NCA-AIIO exam, a candidate encounters a difficult question and has already spent 2 minutes on it without reaching an answer. According to best test-taking practices, what should the candidate do?

A) Continue working on the question until finding the correct answer
B) Skip the question entirely and never return to it
C) Mark the question for review, select the best current answer, and move on
D) Randomly guess and move on without marking for review

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct: C**

Marking the question for review while selecting the best current answer ensures no question is left blank if time runs out, while allowing the candidate to return if time permits. Option A risks running out of time for remaining questions. Option B wastes the opportunity to potentially answer correctly later. Option D foregoes the chance to reconsider with fresh perspective.

</details>

B) Consider that one of the opposite answers is likely correct

### True / False

**Q6.** On the NCA-AIIO exam, all questions carry equal weight, so spending excessive time on difficult questions early in the exam is an effective strategy. — **True / False**

*False. While all questions may carry equal weight, spending excessive time on difficult questions early is NOT an effective strategy. This approach risks running out of time for easier questions later in the exam. The recommended approach is to answer questions you know quickly, mark difficult questions for review, and return to challenging questions only after completing the rest of the exam.*

### Scenario-Based

**Q10.** Maria is taking the NCA-AIIO exam and has 15 minutes remaining with 12 questions left to answer. She notices that 5 of these questions are marked for review because she found them difficult earlier. She has not yet attempted the remaining 7 questions. Considering optimal test-taking strategy, what should Maria do to maximize her score?

A) Spend all 15 minutes on the 5 marked questions since she already attempted the others
B) Quickly answer the 7 unattempted questions first, then return to the 5 marked questions with remaining time
C) Randomly guess on all 12 questions to ensure she finishes
D) Focus only on the 7 unattempted questions and leave the marked questions with her original guesses

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct: B**

Maria should first quickly answer the 7 unattempted questions to ensure all questions have a response. With approximately 75 seconds per question (15 minutes ÷ 12 questions), she has time to attempt everything. After answering the new questions, she can return to the 5 marked questions with any remaining time to reconsider her answers. Option A ignores unattempted questions entirely. Option C wastes the opportunity for thoughtful responses when time still permits. Option D abandons the chance to improve on difficult questions.

</details>
