### Common Patterns
**Pattern 1: Scenario-Based Questions**
Focus on WHY NVIDIA recommends specific solutions; understand use cases, not just features.

**Pattern 2: Trap Answer Recognition**
Eliminate answers that are technically correct but don't fit the specific scenario context.

**Pattern 3: Elimination Strategy**
Remove obviously wrong answers first, then compare remaining options against scenario requirements.

### Things to Remember
✅ Prioritize AI Infrastructure (40%) and Essential AI (38%) in your study plan
✅ Focus on judgment and application — understand the "why" behind recommendations
✅ Flag difficult questions and return later — don't exceed 72 seconds initially
✅ Complete Certiverse platform setup and environment check before exam day
❌ Don't memorize facts without understanding scenario application
