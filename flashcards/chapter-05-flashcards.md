### Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Remote Proctoring | Exam supervision conducted online through webcam and screen monitoring |
| Domain Weight | The percentage each topic area contributes to the total exam score |
| Scenario-Based Reasoning | Questions presenting real-world situations requiring applied knowledge |
| Certiverse | The online platform used to deliver and administer the NCA-AIIO exam |
| Trap Answers | Incorrect options designed to seem correct to test-takers with shallow understanding |
| Recertification | The process of renewing certification after the 2-year validity period expires |
| Digital Badge | A verifiable online credential awarded upon passing the certification exam |

### Memory Tricks
- **"50 in 60"**: Remember 50 questions in 60 minutes—easy numbers that rhyme
- **"40-38-22 Rule"**: AI Infrastructure leads (40), Essential AI follows close (38), Operations trails (22)—descending order like a countdown
- **"72-Second Sprint"**: Think of each question as a quick sprint—just over a minute per question, don't marathon any single question
- **"Why Not What"**: The exam tests WHY NVIDIA recommends solutions, not WHAT features exist—focus on reasoning over memorization
